## TL;DR

**US film studios are publicly demanding Seedance 2.0 be shut down while quietly paying $2M each through offshore entities for exclusive access.** This is the most surreal moment in AI video in 2026.

## What's Happening

BytePlus (ByteDance's enterprise services brand) is selling enterprise-tier exclusive access to Seedance 2.0 at a **$2 million commitment per studio**. What buyers get:

- **Zero queue times** — dedicated compute, no sharing with the public
- **Real-face uploads** — no content moderation restrictions
- **Enterprise SLA** — production-grade reliability

The catch: US studios can't buy directly. With senators demanding ByteDance shut the model down entirely and the MPA sending 7 cease-and-desist letters, **American companies are creating legal entities outside the US and Canada** to route around the political and legal minefield.

> The same studios publicly demanding the product be shut down are paying $2M through shell companies to use it.

## Europe Is Next

The South China Morning Post reported on March 29 that ByteDance confirmed Seedance 2.0 will become available to CapCut users in:

- 🇪🇺 **Europe** (first time explicitly named)

Previous rollouts only covered developing markets. **Europe being named suggests ByteDance's EU AI Act compliance work is further along than most assumed.** The US remains conspicuously absent from any expansion plan.

## Veo 4 Might Not Make Google I/O

The rumor circulating among people tracking Google's AI video roadmap: **Veo 4 will not ship at Google I/O in May** if the team can't match Seedance 2.0's quality. Google's latest actual product move was making Veo 3/3.1 available in Google Ads Asset Studio for image-to-video ad creative — that's iterating on an existing model, not shipping a new one.

Seedance 2.0 still holds the #1 spot on Artificial Analysis in both text-to-video and image-to-video. If Google shows up at I/O with an incremental Veo 3 update instead of a genuine next-gen model, the gap only widens.
