## Background: LLM's Invisible Memory Crisis

Every time you chat with an LLM, the model maintains a **KV Cache** (key-value cache). Longer context = bigger cache. An 8B-parameter model at 32K context burns roughly **4.6 GB of VRAM** on KV cache alone.

On March 24, 2026, Google Research published **TurboQuant** (ICLR 2026), claiming 6x KV cache compression, 8x inference speedup, and zero accuracy loss. Memory stocks crashed the same day.

But Google only published the paper. Code? None.

- `half4` vectorized butterfly operations
- Graph-side rotation
- Block-32 storage layout

## Beyond the Paper: Original Research

Tom didn't stop at reproduction. He added three original improvements on top of Google's work:

### 1. Sparse V (Sparse Values)
Skip **90%** of value decompressions at long context lengths. A sharp observation — not every token's value needs full decompression during inference.

### 2. Asymmetric K/V Quantization
Keep keys precise, compress values harder. The intuition is sound: keys determine *where to look* (attention routing), values determine *what you see* — the former is more precision-sensitive.

### 3. Temporal Decay
Old tokens automatically get lower precision. The farther a token is from the current position, the fewer bits it gets. This mirrors how human attention decays with distance.

## Why This Matters

This isn't just a "talented developer reproduces paper" story. There are several important signals here:

**1. The Real Capability Boundary of AI-Assisted Coding**
Tom explicitly said he used Claude for the implementation. But the key word is "used" — he understood the math, the systems, and the optimization landscape. AI is a tool, not a replacement. The chain looks like: understand the paper math → direct AI to write code → optimize what AI produces. Human judgment remains the core link.

**2. Paper-to-Production Velocity Is Accelerating Exponentially**
Academic papers used to take months or years to become usable implementations. Now one person + AI tools can do it in 7 days. The implications for ML community cadence are profound.

**3. Consumer Hardware Possibilities Are Expanding**
4-bit quantized model + 4-bit TurboQuant KV cache = large models on consumer GPUs. A year ago this was impractical. Now a MacBook runs 35B parameters.
