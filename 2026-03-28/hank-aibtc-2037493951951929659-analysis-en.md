# Deep-Live-Cam: Real-Time Face Swapping With a Single Photo

## What Is Deep-Live-Cam?

HankAI highlighted an open-source project called **Deep-Live-Cam** that has exploded to **81.5k+ GitHub stars** — and for good reason. With just a single photo, you can swap your face in real-time on a webcam. This isn't a post-processing trick. It runs **live**, directly usable in video calls.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Real-time face swapping** — Runs directly on your webcam feed. Works with livestreams, Zoom, Discord, and other video platforms.
2. **Single image input** — No model training, no datasets. Just pick a face, select your camera, and hit start.
3. **Multi-face support** — Can handle multiple people in the same scene simultaneously.
4. **Cross-platform + hardware acceleration** — Supports NVIDIA, AMD, and Apple Silicon with GPU acceleration for smooth performance.
5. **Production-quality output** — Built-in face enhancement, lip-sync, and blending optimization that's a step above most demos.

## Why This Matters for Builders

This project isn't just "another deepfake tool." It represents a fundamental shift:

- **Zero technical barrier**: No ML expertise required. No training data. Fully plug-and-play.
- **Real-time changes everything**: Moving from "post-production" to "live interaction" opens entirely new (and dangerous) application spaces.
- **Open-source means unstoppable**: The code is out there. Anyone can fork, modify, and deploy.

## The Ethics Question

The project maintainers have stated clear guidelines:
- No inappropriate content
- Real person consent required
- Watermarking and feature restrictions may apply

But the reality is that this is **a domain where the technology has matured faster than the rules**. For teams building identity verification, KYC, or security products, this project is a wake-up call: **face-based verification may no longer be reliable**. The person on a video call might not be who they appear to be.
