## The Founder Story: From $2B Exit to Starting Over

Sam Kroonenburg isn't your average repeat founder.

In 2015, he founded **A Cloud Guru** in Melbourne — a cloud computing education platform. Six years later in 2021, Pluralsight acquired the company for approximately **$2 billion**. It was one of the largest tech acquisitions in Australian history.

Most people would retire to a beach after a $2B exit. Sam chose to start again in 2023, this time targeting a space he believed AI could fundamentally transform: **advertising creative production**.

He brought in two co-founders:
- **Jack White** — co-founder of Melbourne ad agency Sunday Gravy, who understands the pain points of the ad industry
- **Ed Ring** — former marketer at Swisse (Australian health supplements giant), who understands what brands actually need

Technology + advertising + brand marketing. That triangle became Cuttable.

## What Does Cuttable Do?

In one sentence: **an AI-powered automated advertising creative platform**.

If you've ever worked in performance marketing, you know the biggest bottleneck isn't budget or targeting strategy — it's **creative production**. A typical performance marketing team needs to:

- Produce large volumes of ad variants weekly (or daily)
- A/B test across different audiences, platforms, and formats
- Rapidly iterate based on data — swap headlines, images, CTAs
- Maintain brand consistency while constantly innovating

This process has traditionally been extremely manual and designer-dependent. Cuttable uses AI to automate it.

Think of it as **"Canva for ads, but AI-powered and laser-focused on performance marketing."**

## Why AI Ad Creative Is Exploding

Ad creative production is a massively underappreciated bottleneck. Several structural trends are driving the opportunity:

**1. Ad platforms increasingly reward creative quality**
Meta, Google, and TikTok's algorithms are highly mature — there's diminishing room for differentiation through targeting alone. The real performance lever is the creative itself.

**2. Personalization demands are exploding**
A single ad needs to work across multiple platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Google Display), multiple formats (vertical, horizontal, square), and multiple audience segments. The combinatorial explosion means creative demand is growing exponentially.

**3. Designer capacity can't keep up**
Good designers are expensive and scarce. Having them produce mechanical ad variants is a waste of talent. AI can handle 80% of the variant work, freeing designers to focus on creative direction.

**4. Measurable ROI**
Performance marketing's greatest advantage is that everything is quantifiable. 13x ROAS isn't a pitch deck promise — it's real advertising data backed by real spend.
